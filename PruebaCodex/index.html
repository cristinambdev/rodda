You agree to use the service lawfully, provide accurate information when you contact a shelter, and treat other users and shelter teams respectfully. You must be old enough to enter into an agreement where you live, or use the service with a responsible parent or guardian.

      2. Dog listings and shelter information

      Listings are provided by participating shelters or rescue organizations. They are responsible for the accuracy and completeness of their listing details, including a dog’s age, health, behavior, availability, and adoption requirements. A listing or saved like does not reserve a dog or guarantee that the dog is available.

      3. Adoption contacts

      Adoption applications, screening, fees, and final decisions are handled directly by the relevant shelter or rescue organization. GoodPup does not make adoption decisions and is not a party to an adoption. Contact the organization for its current process and requirements.

      4. Safety and responsible adoption

      Take reasonable care when communicating or arranging a meeting. Confirm the identity of the shelter, meet in a setting arranged with the organization, and do not send money or sensitive information unless you have independently confirmed the request is legitimate. Consider your household, finances, and ability to care for a dog before applying.

      5. Information and privacy

      This prototype may store dogs you like in your browser so they remain available during later visits on that browser. In a future service, information such as profile details, location, and messages may be used to provide and improve adoption discovery. Do not enter sensitive personal information into this prototype. A real service would need a separate, complete privacy notice describing its actual data practices.

      The service and listings are provided as available. Features may change, be interrupted, or be removed, and GoodPup may update listing or service information. These sample terms do not promise uninterrupted service or a particular adoption outcome.
